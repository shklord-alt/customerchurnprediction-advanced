HOW A SCAN WORKS

From account details to a risk verdict

INPUT

Account details

Contract type, tenure, billing method, and the services the customer has active.

MODEL

Gradient Boosting classifier

Trained and cross-validated on 7,043 historical accounts; selected over logistic regression and random forest on held-out ROC-AUC.

OUTPUT

Probability + risk band

A churn probability from 0–100%, bucketed into stable / watch / elevated / critical, plus the top factors behind the score.

CONTEXT

Where they sit in the base

The 3D orbit view places the account among a sample of real customers by tenure, monthly charges, and total spend.
